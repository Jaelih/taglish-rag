Pag-IBIG Title Release

Know how to claim your title upon full payment of your Pag-IBIG Fund Housing Loan

Requirements

Choose which type of claimant you are:

Registered Owner

Accomplished Home Financing Documents Claim Stub Form
Two (2) valid IDs with signature and photo
See list of Valid IDs
Marriage Contract (for female registered owner who was single at point of loan application but is already married at point of claim of title)

A Representative

Accomplished Home Financing Documents Claim Stub Form
Two (2) valid IDs with signature and photo
Special Power of Attorney executed by the registered owner, see details below :
If registered owner is an OFW or residing outside the country - SPA should be duly notarized prior to date of departure or duly certified and authenticated by the Philippine Embassy or Consulate in the country where the registered owner is staying
If regsitered owner is physically incapacitated - medical certificate

Heirs of the Deceased Registered Owner

Accomplished Home Financing Documents Claim Stub Form
Two (2) valid IDs with signature and photo each of the registered owner and heir/s
PSA Certified True Copy of Borrower's Death Certificate
PSA Certified True Copy of Birth Certificate/s of the heir/s of deceased registered Owner
PSA Certified True Copy of Birth Certificate/s of siblings, if deceased registered owner is single and parents are deceased
Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) and PSA
Certified True Copy of Marriage Contract of Parents, for single registered owner PSA Certified True Copy of Marriage Contract, for married registered owner

Assignee/Successor in Interest

Accomplished Home Financing Documents Claim Stub Form
Two (2) valid IDs with signature and photo each of the registered owner and heir/s
Photocopy of the following (if applicable):
PSA Certified True Copy of Borrower's Death Certificate
PSA Certified True Copy of Birth Certificate/s of the heir/s of deceased registered Owner
PSA Certified True Copy of Birth Certificate/s of siblings, if deceased registered owner is single and parents are deceased
Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) and PSA Certified True Copy of Marriage Contract of Parents, for single registered owner
PSA Certified True Copy of Marriage Contract, for married registered owner
Notarized Affidavit of Guardianship, for heir/s 18 years old and below, or physically/mentally incompetent (if applicable)
Notarized Proof of Surviving Heir/s
SPA executed by the heir/s of deceased registered owner
Deed of Partition/Last Will and Testament, Final Decision on Intestate/Estate Proceedings of deceased registered owner, if applicable
Deed of Sale/Assignment executed by the deceased registered owner in favor of the claimant
SPA executed by the surviving spouse and other heir/s of the deceased registered owner
