Why Choose the Short-Term Loan for Home Improvement?
Borrow up to ₱300,000​

Get the funds you need for renovations, repairs, or minor construction, specifically those that do not require a building permit.

Minimal
Documents​

Enjoy easy documentation and minimal paperworks.The loan can also include the cost of annotating the Deed of Negative Pledge and the Documentary Stamp Tax, so there’s no extra hassle on your part. ​

Low Interest & Flexible Terms

Benefit from a low fixed interest rate and ​Flexible payment terms of up to 5 years.​

Qualifications Requirements Forms Steps to Apply
Qualifications

You are eligible to apply for this loan if you:

Are an active *Pag-IBIG member with at least 12 monthly savings
*You are considered an active member if you have made at least one (1) monthly savings contribution within the last 6 months before applying for the loan​
Are 65 years old or younger at the time of application and not older than 70 at the end of your loan term;​​
Have passed Pag-IBIG’s background, credit, and employment/business checks;​​
Have no Pag-IBIG loan in arrears, and no previous Pag-IBIG housing account that was foreclosed, cancelled, or voluntarily surrendered;​​
Own the property you want to improve and make sure it is free from any legal claims, debts, or restrictions (liens and encumbrances) for the entire duration of the loan.​
Requirements

Prepare the following documents:

Accomplished Short-Term Loan for Home Improvement Application Form
One (1) valid ID
See list of Valid IDs
Proof of Income
For Locally Employed Individuals
For Self-Employed Individuals
For Overseas Filipino Workers (OFW)
Deed of Negative Pledge with Promissory Note
Updated Real Estate Tax Receipt (as of the latest quarter before application)​​
Colored pictures of exterior​
Vicinity map/sketch of the location of property showing landmarks​
Other documentary requirements as may be required by our branches​
Steps to Apply

IF YOU WILL SUBMIT YOUR APPLICATION IN ANY OF OUR BRANCHES:
Step 1:

Prepare your filled out Short-Term Loan for Home Improvement Application Form with other requirements and submit it to the nearest Pag-IBIG Fund branch.
