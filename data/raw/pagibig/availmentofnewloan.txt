Why Choose the Pag-IBIG Housing Loan?
Higher loanable amount

Loan up to ₱10 Million per member to achieve your dream home.

You may also add up to two co-borrowers.

Various Loan Purposes

Borrow up to ₱10 million to buy, build, or renovate your home.

Available for house and lot, condominium, and lot purchase.

Longer Payment Terms

Enjoy a longer payment period of up to 30 years to make your monthly payments more affordable.
