Why Choose the Pag-IBIG Multi-Purpose Loan?

The cash loan for your immediate financial needs.

Fast Approval

Borrow up to 90% of your Pag-IBIG Regular Savings and get your loan in as fast as 3 days.

Flexible Payment Terms

Pay your loan in 12, 24 or 36 easy monthly installments.

Easy Loan Renewal

Renew your loan even only after 4 monthly payments.

Qualifications
Must be an active Pag-IBIG member with at least one (1) monthly contribution within the last six (6) months
At least twelve (12) monthly membership savings under the Pag-IBIG Regular Savings;
If with an existing Multi-Purpose Loan, Calamity Loan or HELPs, the account/s must not be in default;

Accomplished Pag-IBIG Multi-Purpose Loan Application Form
