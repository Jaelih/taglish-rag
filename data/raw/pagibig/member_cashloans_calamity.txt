Pag-IBIG Calamity Loan

Get the cash loan for your emergency needs at only 5.95% interest rate per year.

Lowest Interest Rate in the Market: Borrow up to 90% of your savings with a low interest rate. Get your loan in as fast as 3 days. Pay your loan in 12, 24 or 36 easy monthly installments, with a 3-month grace period.

Qualifications: Must be an active Pag-IBIG member with at least one (1) monthly contribution within the last six (6) months; at least twelve (12) monthly membership savings under the Pag-IBIG Regular Savings; if with an existing Multi-Purpose Loan, Calamity Loan, or HELPs, the account/s must not be in default; residing or working in an area declared under a State of Calamity by the LGU or national government; must apply within ninety (90) days from the official declaration of a State of Calamity in your area.

Requirements: Accomplished Pag-IBIG Calamity Loan Application Form; One (1) valid ID; Proof of Income (varies for locally employed, self-employed, OFW); Cash Card; if applying online via Virtual Pag-IBIG, a selfie photo showing your valid ID and cash card.

Steps to Apply: At any Branch - accomplish the Calamity Loan Application Form and submit with other requirements. Online via the Virtual Pag-IBIG.
