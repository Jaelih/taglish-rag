Pag-IBIG Home Saver Programs
Do you have missed housing loan payments for 3 to 9 months?

Your options are:

Loan Restructuring
Plan of Payment
Penalty Condonation

Do you have missed housing loan payments for more than 9 months?

Your options are:

Housing Loan Restructuring
Housing Loan Revaluation
Plan of Payment
Penalty Condonation

Learn more about each program below

Housing Loan Restructuring

Spread your unpaid monthly amortizations across your remaining loan term. You may also reduce your monthly payment by lengthening your loan term.

Eligibility

Open to ALL Housing Loan borrowers, including beneficiaries of GLAD Program, except the following:

Housing loans with cancelled Contract-to-Sell (CTS
Foreclosed accounts that are already sold to another individual
Foreclosed accounts that are already owned by Pag-IBIG Fund
Interest rate shall be based on Pag-IBIG Fund’s Full-Risk Based Pricing Framework for restructured loans
Maximum loan term: 30 years, but should not exceed the difference between borrower's age and age 70

Waive ALL penalties and additional interests recorded by fully updating or fully paying your housing loan account. We will charge ZERO processing fees.

Eligibility
