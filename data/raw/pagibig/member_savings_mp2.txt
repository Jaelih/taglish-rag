Return Rates

The MP2 Savings, as a voluntary savings program, earns higher annual returns than the mandatory Pag-IBIG Regular Savings.

Here are the MP2 Savings Return Rates since 2021

Year

Return Rates

2025 7.12%
2024 7.10%
2023 7.05%
2022 7.03%
2021 6.00%

Under our Charter, we are mandated to return at least 70% of our annual net income as dividends and returns to our Members’ Savings accounts.
These amounts are automatically credited to your MP2 Savings every year.

This means that the more you save, the higher the returns

Open an MP2 Savings Account

Be an active Pag-IBIG member to open your own MP2 Savings Account.

An active Pag-IBIG member must have made at least one (1) monthly contribution within the last six (6) months.
