Claim your Regular Savings

Get your full membership savings with dividends.

Reason for Regular Savings Claim - Maturity
Qualification: Maturity Claims for Pag-IBIG members who have remitted a total of 240 monthly membership savings.
Requirements: Accomplished Application for Provident Benefits (APB) Claim Form; Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card or Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card Plus; One (1) valid ID of the member; SSS employment history (if applicable).

Optional withdrawal for Regular Savings - 15 years
Qualification: Member who has completed 180 continuous monthly savings.
Requirements: Accomplished Application for Provident Benefits (APB) Claim Form (1 Original); Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card/Loyalty Card Plus; One (1) valid ID of the member (1 Photocopy).

Steps to Apply for Regular Savings Claim - Maturity: At any Branch - Accomplish the APB Claim Form and submit, along with the other requirements, to the Pag-IBIG branch nearest you. Online via the Virtual Pag-IBIG - Prepare scanned copy or photo of the accomplished APB Claim Form and supporting documents.

Reason for Regular Savings Claim - Retirement
Compulsory Retirement: 65 years old and above. Optional Retirement: 60 years old.
Qualifications: Retirement Claims for Pag-IBIG members who have reached retiring age.
Requirements: Accomplished APB Claim Form; Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card or Loyalty Card Plus; One (1) valid ID of the member; SSS employment history (if applicable); Order of Retirement (For AFP/PNP/BJMP/BFP members); Statement of Service (For AFP); Service Record (For PNP/BJMP/BFP); Certificate of Early Retirement (private employees at least 45 years old); GSIS Retirement Voucher (government employees).

Death claims (If married with children / If single with children / If single without children): each requires the Accomplished APB Claim Form plus PSA-issued Death Certificate, Birth/Marriage Certificates as applicable, Proof of Surviving Legal Heirs, Affidavit/Declaration of guardianship for minor children, valid IDs of claimant/witnesses/beneficiaries, and (for single members) a Joint Affidavit of Cohabitation.

Other Grounds: Permanent Departure from the Country (migrating members), Expatriates (with Pag-IBIG contributions), Permanent Total Disability (PTD) or Insanity, Critical Illness (of member or immediate family), Termination from Service by Reason of Health -- each with its own APB Claim Form plus supporting medical/immigration documents.
