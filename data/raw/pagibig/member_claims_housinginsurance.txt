Housing Loan Insurance

Claim your Housing Loan Insurance if your house is damaged by fire or natural calamities.

Housing Loan Insurance - Non-Life
Qualification: A Pag-IBIG Fund housing loan borrower whose properties are affected by a calamity or other cause of loss, and/or who are entitled to insurance claims due to Policy riders and other coverage extended by the Insurance Broker, shall file their insurance claims within six (6) months from the occurrence thereof. The housing loan borrower may submit a Report of Loss to Pag-IBIG Fund prior to submission of complete documentary requirements.

Requirements: Accomplished Application of Non-Life Insurance Claim Form; Two valid IDs with signature; Cost of Damages/Bill of Materials (Total loss - signed by Contractor; Partial damage - signed by Foreman or a Carpenter); Report of Loss; Colored pictures of the damaged property or any proof of loss/damage. For Fire Claims: Over P200,000 - BFP Certificate and/or Incident Form; Up to P200,000 - Barangay Certification.

Steps to Apply: via Pag-IBIG Housing Hub or Business Center - get a queue number and wait to be called. via Email - accomplish and submit Report of Loss form, then upon notification submit the Application for Non-Life Insurance Claims via contactus@pagibigfund.gov.ph.
