Reason for MP2 Savings Claim - Maturity
Qualification: MP2 Maturity Claims for Pag-IBIG members who have reached 5 years membership savings.
Requirements: Accomplished Application for Provident Benefits (APB) Claim Form; Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card or Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card Plus; One (1) valid ID of the member.
Steps to Apply: At any Branch - Accomplish the APB Claim Form and submit, along with the other requirements, to the Pag-IBIG branch nearest you. Online via the Virtual Pag-IBIG - Prepare scanned copy or photo of the accomplished APB Claim Form and supporting documents.

Death claims (If married with children / If single with children / If single without children): each requires the Accomplished APB Claim Form plus PSA-issued Death Certificate, Birth/Marriage Certificates as applicable, Proof of Surviving Legal Heirs (HQP-PFF-030), Affidavit/Declaration of guardianship for minor children (HQP-PFF-028), valid IDs of claimant/witnesses/beneficiaries, and (for single members) a Joint Affidavit of Cohabitation.

Other Grounds: Permanent Departure from the Country, Expatriates, Permanent Total Disability (PTD) or Insanity, Critical Illness (of member or immediate family), Termination from Service by Reason of Health -- each with its own APB Claim Form plus supporting documents.
