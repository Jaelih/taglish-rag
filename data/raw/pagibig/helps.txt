Pag-IBIG Health and Education Loan Programs (HELPs)

Secure your health and education needs today. No Processing Fee. Bigger discounts from partner schools and hospitals.

Qualifications: Must be an active Pag-IBIG member with at least one (1) monthly contribution within the last six (6) months; at least twelve (12) monthly membership savings under the Pag-IBIG Regular Savings; if with an existing Multi-Purpose Loan, Calamity Loan, or HELPs, the account/s must not be in default; must avail loan from Pag-IBIG HELPs partner schools and/or hospitals.

Requirements: Accomplished Pag-IBIG HELPs Application Form; Billing assessment from the HELPs partner school or hospital; One (1) valid ID; Proof of Income (varies for locally employed, self-employed, OFW).

Steps to apply: At any Branch - visit the nearest Pag-IBIG Branch to get your HELPs evaluation.
